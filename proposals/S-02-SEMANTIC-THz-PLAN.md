## Research Questions
1. How can semantic intent replace raw bit transmission to reduce THz bandwidth consumption?
2. What VAE-based distributed architecture enables semantic reconstruction at the receiver?
3. What is the semantic fidelity vs. SNR tradeoff in molecularly attenuated THz channels?

## Methodology
- **Channel Model:** THz propagation with molecular absorption (water vapor distributions)
- **Semantic Encoding:** Distributed VAE for rate-distortion optimization of semantic content
- **Reconstruction:** Semantic reconstruction at receiver with conceptual fidelity metrics
- **Evaluation:** Compare semantic transmission vs raw bit transmission under same bandwidth
- **Metrics:** Semantic fidelity, SNR, bandwidth efficiency, reconstruction quality

## Key Components
```
Semantic Source → VAE Encoder → THz Channel (molecular absorption)
                                      │
                                      ▼
                              VAE Decoder → Semantic Reconstruction
                                      │
                                      ▼
                              Fidelity Metrics
```
