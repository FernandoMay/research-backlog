## Research Questions
1. How can electromagnetic scattering characteristics in sub-THz bands encode covert transmissions?
2. What is the detectability threshold of covert signals embedded in phase-noise profiles?
3. How robust are physical-layer covert channels against interceptors with varying capabilities?

## Methodology
- **Signal Design:** Embed covert signals in sub-THz electromagnetic scattering profiles
- **Phase-Noise Inversion:** Use transceiver phase-noise as cover signal
- **Detection Theory:** Stochastic entropy metrics + KL divergence for detection threshold
- **Analysis:** Near-field vs far-field interceptor capability analysis
- **Metrics:** Covert capacity, detection probability, bit error rate, covertness index
