## Research Questions
1. How can a cognitive layer select resources, routes, and THz configurations under dynamic constraints?
2. What quantum-inspired optimization outperforms classical methods for multi-objective THz resource allocation?
3. How does the integration of XING cognitive runtime with quantum optimization improve network resilience?

## Methodology
- **Architecture:** XING Cognitive Runtime → Cognitive Resilience Layer → Quantum-Inspired Optimizer
- **Optimization:** Quantum-inspired algorithms for power, spectrum, and routing optimization
- **Simulation:** THz-6G network scenario with dynamic traffic and channel conditions
- **Comparison:** Classical PSO/GA vs quantum-inspired vs hybrid approaches
- **Metrics:** Resource utilization, latency, throughput, resilience under faults
