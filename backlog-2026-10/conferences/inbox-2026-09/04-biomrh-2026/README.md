# BioMRH 2026 — 2nd International Symposium on BioMechatronics and Robotics in Healthcare

**Classification: `Verified thin`**

The email supplied no website. An official site **does** exist at `https://www.biomrh.cc/`, found by search. It corroborates location, dates, edition number, deadline, OpenConf submission and Springer LNNS. Third-party caches of the same URL still show a July 22, 2026 deadline, so the site was updated after those caches were taken.

This is a second edition. One prior edition, in Brussels in 2025, is claimed on the site.
