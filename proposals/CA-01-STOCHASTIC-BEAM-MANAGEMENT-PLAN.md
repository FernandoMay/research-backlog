## Research Questions
1. How can stochastic game theory model beam management in dynamic THz swarms?
2. What multi-agent RL strategies optimize beam alignment under path loss and energy constraints?
3. How does the framework perform compared to centralized beam management approaches?

## Methodology
- **Game Model:** Stochastic Markov game formulation for beam alignment
- **Optimization:** Multi-Agent RL (MARL) + PSO estocástico for distributed decisions
- **Scenario:** THz beam management with mobility, blockage, and energy constraints
- **Simulation:** Multi-agent environment with 10+ agents, dynamic topology
- **Metrics:** Beam alignment accuracy, energy consumption, latency, throughput
