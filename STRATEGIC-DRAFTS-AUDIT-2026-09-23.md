# Strategic Drafts Audit — 2026-09-23

## Decision

The supplied compilation is a useful research portfolio draft, but its numerical tables are not yet reproducible from the current repositories. It must be treated as a planning document until each experiment has executable code, public data provenance, fixed seeds, baselines, and generated outputs.

## Research Lines

| Draft | Venue | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Fuzzy-calibrated uncertainty | ISCMI 2026 | Develop first; public tabular/time-series benchmark |
| Semantic JSCC over mobile edge networks | INCC 2026 | Develop after dataset/channel audit; reuse SEM-01 infrastructure |
| Spatial-temporal Transformer swarm for BCI | ICCIT 2026 | Highest risk; requires subject-wise public EEG evaluation |

## Claims to Withhold Until Reproduced

- MIMIC-III/UCI ECE and Brier tables.
- 72% bandwidth reduction and 95.4% task accuracy.
- 86.9% BCI accuracy, kappa 0.82, and sub-10-ms inference.

Every number must be generated by the new repository and archived with command, seed, environment, dataset hash, and raw metrics.

## Reuse Boundaries

- `qce-ieee-package` is assigned/submitted to ICITES and is not reusable as the same ETCM manuscript.
- COGN-NDAN is a CINC/WITCOM lineage; new BCI papers must disclose prior work and introduce a distinct contribution.
- Existing six conference packages are baselines, not evidence that new venue submissions are experimentally complete.
