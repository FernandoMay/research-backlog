## Research Questions
1. How can quantum-inspired algorithms optimize the sensing-communication tradeoff in ISAC waveforms?
2. What Pareto-optimal waveform designs emerge from quantum simulated annealing?
3. How does ISAC performance compare under quantum-inspired vs classical optimization?

## Methodology
- **Formulation:** Multi-objective optimization for sensing resolution vs communication rate
- **Algorithm:** Quantum Simulated Annealing for waveform synthesis
- **Waveform:** OFDM/OTFS hybrid optimized dynamically
- **Evaluation:** Joint radar sensing + data transmission in THz band
- **Metrics:** Sensing resolution, data rate (Gbps), waveform efficiency, convergence
