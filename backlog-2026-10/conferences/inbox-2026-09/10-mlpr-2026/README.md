# MLPR 2026 — 4th International Conference on Machine Learning and Pattern Recognition

**Classification: `Verified thin`**

The email said no deadline was stated. The official site states one: 30 September 2026. Location, dates, edition number and contact all match.

The recommended journal was verified independently and is real, but three of its claims do not hold up: the site says `SCI` where the journal's own pages say `ESCI`, the $1,800 APC discount is a fast-track-for-payment mechanism, and the "published linkage" URL on the conference site points to an unrelated journal's article.

## Discrepancies

| Discrepancy | Source A | Source B | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Journal indexing level | MLPR site: `SCI` | IR's own pages: `ESCI`, accepted September 2024 | **Material overstatement by the conference site.** ESCI is not the Science Citation Index Expanded |
| Q2 claim | MLPR site: `Q2` | IR history: Q2 in Automation & Control Systems, **Q3** in Computer Science, AI | The label is true only in one of two categories. A BCI or multi-agent paper would be assessed in the Computer Science category |
| Impact factor year and value | MLPR site: `3.9` | SciJournal (third-party): 4.130 for 2025, 3.100 for 2024 | Two different third-party positionings. The journal's own claim is 3.9 (2025), up from 2.3 (2024). Recorded as an unresolved disagreement between the publisher and a third-party database |
| "Published linkage" citation | MLPR site cites an article URL as evidence of the publication path | The URL is a liver-surgery meeting abstract in an unrelated journal | **The example citation is wrong.** It does not demonstrate an MLPR-to-IR publication. Recorded as a broken evidentiary claim on the conference site |
| Deadline | Email omitted | Site news shows an extension to 20 August 2026, then the current 30 September 2026 date | Not a contradiction. The site shows successive values; the current one is 30 September 2026 |
