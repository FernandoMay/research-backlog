## Research Questions
1. What formal model captures the evolution from NDAN to XING as layers of a unified cognitive architecture?
2. How do NDAN, CRE, and XING relate as capability layers rather than independent systems?
3. What taxonomy of distributed cognitive intelligence emerges from this evolution?

## Methodology
- **Taxonomy:** Classify cognitive system capabilities into layered taxonomy
- **Formal Model:** Capability algebra mapping NDAN → CRE → XING transitions
- **Architecture:** Define unified model with semantic, cognitive, and infrastructure layers
- **Comparison:** Compare with existing multi-agent and cognitive architectures
- **Prototype:** Demonstrate unified model using XING implementation
