# ICETC 2026 — 2026 the 18th International Conference on Education Technology and Computers

**Classification: `Contradicted`**

The email states a submission deadline of `November 10, 2026`. The official site is internally inconsistent: the homepage "Important Dates" block says `October 30, 2026`, while the site's own news item of August 31, 2026 says the deadline "extends to `October 10, 2026`". No official page publishes November 10. Every other checked fact (edition, city, dates, publisher, contact) matches.
